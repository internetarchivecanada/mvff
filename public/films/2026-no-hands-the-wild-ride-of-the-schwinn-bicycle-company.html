No Hands: The Wild Ride of the Schwinn Bicycle Company

From the festival

No Hands: The Wild Ride of the Schwinn Bicycle Company is exactly that. It's a wild ride about an enduring American brand; it's a history of our nation, of the world, and of a family throughout the last century. This testament to the endurance of the American dream, narrated by Lance Armstrong, is both nostalgic and edifying — a unique look at how one company's fortunes rose and fell alongside the country's own — but the film really shines when the spotlight turns to a group of Marin County mountain bikers à la Robin Williams who screech onto the scene, kicking up dust and showing us how the real ruff-riders roll. This is a local film with international insight that'll have you getting misty over banana seats or eyeing that old 10-speed out in the garage and fantasizing about riding it down the south side of Mount Tam. —Claire Holl
