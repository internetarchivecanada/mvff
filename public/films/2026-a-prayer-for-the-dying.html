A Prayer for the Dying

From the festival

Play Trailer Dara Van Dusen’s feature debut is a visually compelling Western set in 1870 in Friendship, Wisconsin. In the settlement—a place with not much more than a general store, a church, and a handful of smallholdings—everyone ekes out the barest of existences, including Jacob (Johnny Flynn), the town’s sheriff, preacher, and undertaker. When an outbreak of diphtheria threatens the community’s stability, he and the village doctor (John C. Reilly) navigate the threat as best they know how: initially, by concealing it, hoping it will subside. But Jacob finds himself torn between his roles as a leader, a husband and father, and a Civil War veteran navigating the haunting memories of his service, as another threat, a wildfire, looms on the horizon. With striking cinematography and sound design, Van Dusen creates a world both familiar and eerily apocalyptic, driven by great central performances. It’s a notable achievement for a first-time filmmaker. —Zoë Elton
