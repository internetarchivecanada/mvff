Bamboozled

From the festival

Play Trailer Our celebration of Spike Lee’s amazing career continues with this special screening, chosen by the director. Bamboozled satirizes popular culture and adds dance legend Savion Glover’s muscular tapping to the mix to create an indelible film that has only grown in power in the 25 years since its release. Tommy Davidson and Glover star as Womack and Manray, a pair of buskers recruited to star in a modern-day minstrel show in Spike Lee’s scabrous tragicomedy. Unhappy television writer Pierre Delacroix (Damon Wayans) is desperate to get away from his boss Dunwitty (Michael Rapaport) and the man’s many micro and macro aggressions. Pierre can’t quit without breaking his contract, so he seeks to get fired by mounting a flamboyantly racist TV series so offensive it’s bound to fail. When the opposite happens and the show becomes not just a hit but a cultural phenomenon, Womack, Manray, and even Delacroix initially enjoy their success but the reprehensible material and the laughter it engenders begins to eat at their souls. A rare big screen opportunity! —Pam Grady
