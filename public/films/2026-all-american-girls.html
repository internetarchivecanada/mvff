All American Girls

From the festival

If you’ve ever pined for an American Girl doll or its tiny, perfect accessories, this documentary is right in your dollhouse. Director Christy Wegener ( I Am Vanessa Guillen ) pulls back the meticulously crafted curtain on the venture that quietly began as Pleasant Company and vaulted into the multimillion-dollar empire known as American Girl®. Featuring an exclusive interview with founder Pleasant Rowland, along with archival footage and insights from original staffers and diehard fans, this energetic deep dive examines the feminist and academic framework behind the characters and the triumphs and controversies that shaped the booming business. Get the backstory on how Rowland dreamed up the historical dolls and their intricate worlds. Meet the adults who keep aflame the passion that defined their childhoods in the ‘80s, ‘90s, and early 2000s. And revel in feisty memes and multimedia artwork that illuminate the brand’s unparalleled influence and ongoing cultural relevance. —D. Quinones
