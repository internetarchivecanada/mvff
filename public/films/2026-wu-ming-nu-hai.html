Wu Ming Nü Hai

From the festival

Play Trailer Born in the wake of China’s controversial one-child policy, six-year-old Wang Juan (Cao Ruofan) finds her life completely upended by her mother’s pregnancy. The young girl is soon taken from her rural home—where kite-flying and swimming fill her days—and brought to the home of a discordant, childless couple. Abandoned in an unfamiliar city with her new legal guardians, Juan strives to find a sense of belonging, a struggle that only worsens when she’s uprooted twice more before turning 18 years old. Anchored by a powerful performance from Resurrection’s Li Gengxi, who plays teenage Juan, Zou Jing’s confident debut is an achingly tender coming-of-age drama. And while it never shies away from the harrowing realities of the political directive that dehumanized and haunted so many, A Girl Unknown also reaffirms the undeniable, striking hopefulness of a young person’s belief in love and self. —Kate Bove
