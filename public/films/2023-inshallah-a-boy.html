Inshallah A Boy

From the festival

After her husband’s death, Nawal faces the loss of her home in this suspenseful and devastating look at a woman driven to extremes. As her brother-in-law claims ownership of the home due to patriarchal marital laws, Nawal constructs a complex web of secrets and lies to buy some time.
