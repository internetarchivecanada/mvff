2023 (46th festival)

Maestro

From the festival

Bradley Cooper and Carey Mulligan deliver stunning performances as Leonard Bernstein and his wife, actress Felicia Montealegre, in this intimate dramatic portrait of their marriage and a career that made him an indispensable figure in the history of classical music. Along with his extraordinary portrayal, Cooper directs and co-wrote the script.
