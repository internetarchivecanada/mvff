Wicker

From the festival

Play Trailer Playing to the peak of their immaculate powers, Olivia Colman, Alexander Skarsgård, and Peter Dinklage bring grand charm, delight, and whimsy to this altogether unique and fantastical folk romance for the ages. Mocked by the eccentric, coupled townsfolk for her spinsterish manner, a fisherwoman (Colman) in this medieval world that is both wonderful and strange beseeches the local basket maker (Dinklage) to weave her a husband. He does, bringing to life the wicker man (Skarsgård) of her dreams, creating a cascading sensation throughout the village. Lushly designed, carefully crafted, and skillfully paced, this adaptation of Ursula Willis’s beguiling short story generated its own percolating buzz when it premiered at Sundance. Husband-and-wife directing duo Alex Huston Fischer and Eleanor Wilson imbue their fractured tale with the liveliness and wit of master storytellers, weaving their mythical yarn with precision and captivating magic that is all too rare. —Landon Zakheim
