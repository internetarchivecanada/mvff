Rose

From the festival

Play Trailer In the village, they call him a soldier, as well as an outsider and a misfit. Concealing many secrets and a facial scar, he excels at hard work in his quest to become part of the community. But beneath the soldier’s identity is a woman discovering a new sense of freedom and independence by living as a man, possibilities that would otherwise be denied to her. Sandra Hüller ( Anatomy of a Fall , MVFF46) delivers a performance of searing psychological intensity as a 17th-century war veteran. Markus Schleinzer’s third feature, after Michael and Angelo , is a stark historical drama; its striking black-and-white imagery reinforces its questions about gender, identity, and the price of belonging, revealing how simply being perceived as a man can open doors otherwise closed to women. Formally stunning and visually painterly, this human and deeply unsettling portrait turns a historical story into a provocative reflection on freedom, identity, and the lengths we go to claim our own lives. —Ivonne Cotorruelo
