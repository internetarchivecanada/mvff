Querida Fátima

From the festival

Play Trailer A decade after the murder of her 12-year-old daughter, Lorena Gutiérrez refuses to let Fátima's story disappear. Camping outside Mexico's National Palace, she waits for an audience with the country's first female president, carrying a question that has nagged at Lorena for years: What does justice look like when nothing can bring back your child? Lorena is among the directors of this documentary that transforms personal grief into a deeply intimate act of remembrance and resistance. The film moves between Lorena's recollections, her family's struggle, and the stories of other mothers who have also lost daughters to violence, creating a portrait that is as much about love and memory as it is about the search for accountability. Most importantly, Fátima emerges as no mere statistic, but as a girl with dreams, a daughter remembered with tenderness and determination. This triple Guadalajara International Film Festival award winner is a powerful testament to a mother who never imagined becoming an activist but found in that fight a way to keep her daughter's memory alive. - João Federici
