The 9 Lives of Barbara Dane • Performances with Special Guests including Holly Near and Willie Chambers

From the festival

A special one-of-a-kind tribute concert honoring the legendary Barbara Dane and the much-anticipated premiere of Maureen Gosling's captivating documentary, The 9 Lives of Barbara Dane .
