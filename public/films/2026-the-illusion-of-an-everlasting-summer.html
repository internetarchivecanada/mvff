The Illusion of an Everlasting Summer

From the festival

Filmed over 25 years, this intimate documentary casts a tender and fearless eye over the lives of two inseparable cousins, Guillermina and Belinda, as they grow up in the rural Argentine Pampas. Celebrated photographer Alessandra Sanguinetti, in her prize-winning directorial debut, crafts a time capsule as fluid and heartrending as Boyhood and Seven Up! From carefree days playing dress-up and imagining vast futures, the girls mature before our eyes, dancing like no one is watching and ripening under the sun. In a blink, they tip into adulthood, effervescence fading under the weight of responsibility and narrowing options. Like a magic trick, though, their own daughters spring up to reclaim the joy of childhood as the camera keeps rolling. The heart of the narrative is the passage of time, but the film’s real power is how indelibly it captures women’s quiet resilience and the universal language of female friendship. Winner of the Filmmakers of the Present - Golden Leopard Award at the 2026 Locarno International Film Festival. —D. Quinones
