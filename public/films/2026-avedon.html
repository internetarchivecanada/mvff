Avedon

From the festival

Academy Award®-winning director Ron Howard traces the man behind the camera and the iconic images that made Richard Avedon one of the most influential photographers of the 20th century. From the stark white backgrounds that became his signature to his unforgettable portraits of cultural icons like Charlie Chaplin and Marilyn Monroe, Avedon redefined the visual language of fashion and celebrity. Yet beyond the glamour, the film delves into a childhood home built on staged happiness, revealing how those early experiences informed his personal life and artistic vision. With unprecedented access to personal archives, home movies, never-before-seen photographs, and interviews with his son John Avedon, fashion titan Calvin Klein, art dealer Larry Gagosian, and famous muses Isabella Rossellini, Twiggy, and Lauren Hutton, Avedon offers a rare glimpse into the deeply private artist whose extraordinary body of work continues to shape the worlds of photography, fashion, and culture. —Michelle Svenson Justin Wilkes in Conversation Tuesday, October 6, 4:30 PM | SEQ 1
