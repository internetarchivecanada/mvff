Chicas Tristes

From the festival

Fernanda Tovar, in her narrative debut feature, winner of the Crystal Bear for Best Film in the Generation 14plus at the Berlinale, crafts a moving coming-of-age drama about friendship, teenage girlhood, and trauma. Rocio Guzmán gives a standout performance as La Maestra scraping by as best she can to understand how to be a good ally to her friend Paula (Darana Álvarez). Friends and swimmers Paula and La Maestra navigate life with the same confidence they show in the pool. Dreams cast a quiet spell over the early stretches of the film, though its premonitory title, Sad Girlz , suggests that the girls’ carefree world will not remain untouched. One night when they go to a party, something happens to Paula. As their journey deepens and complicates their bond, these two remarkable young women provide a sense of warmth that makes Sad Girlz linger in the heart long after the credits roll. —Ivonne Cotorruelo MVFF49 Mind the Gap Creation Prize Winner
