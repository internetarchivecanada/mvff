The Hypnosis

From the festival

An aspiring entrepreneur and his girlfriend attend a seminar to pitch their new app, not realizing her recent hypnotherapy has severed her inhibitions. An excruciatingly funny cringe comedy, it’s also a touching love story about truly seeing your partner for the first time.
