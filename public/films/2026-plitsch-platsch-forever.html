Plitsch Platsch Forever!

From the festival

Play Trailer Pre-teens Pola and Polly are inseparable friends who spend the long summer days at Splish Splash, the local pool, where Polly’s mom runs the concession stand. When the pool shuts down for lengthy repairs, Polly’s family departs Switzerland for Canada in search of steady work, leaving Pola heartbroken about losing her BFF and angry at her village for closing the coolest spot in town. With the help of Rosalie, the smartest (and geekiest) girl in her class, Pola plots to reopen Splish Splash, recruiting friends—and former enemies—to her side. Her grassroots-style civic activism goes all the way to the top, and even Mayor Petrović-Stucki can’t get the girls off her back or out of her office. Featuring some wildly creative costumes, musical dance numbers, and fabulous young talent, Splish Splash Forever! is an upbeat and infectiously inspiring story of activism, kid-style, and the enduring power of friendship. —KD Davis In German with English subtitles. Appropriate for grades 5-8 (ages 10-13)
