The True Story of Tamara De Lempicka & The Art of Survival

From the festival

One of the defining artists of the 20th century, Tamara de Lempicka was a working mother, feminist, style icon, and bisexual libertine, who embodied Art Deco’s spirit and portrayed women in boldly erotic terms that broke the male gaze.
