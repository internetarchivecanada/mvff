Thelma's Perfect Birthday

From the festival

Thelma, an adventuresome penguin, is four and soon she’ll turn five – she hopes. What if she’s not home when her Birthday arrives? Reinis Kalnaellis brings to life a beautifully animated tale of self-acceptance, friendship and the meaning of growing up.
