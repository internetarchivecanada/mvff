The A-Word: The Future of Aging

From the festival

Play Trailer We’re all familiar with those slick hucksters hawking magical anti-aging elixirs they claim will extend lives and make us look and feel younger. Few, if any, work. Today, though, sees a new dawn emerging as vetted research scientists and innovators—some entrenched in the Bay Area—make significant breakthroughs that have the potential to add “quality” years to our lives. Greg Kohs, award-winning director of the 2008 documentary Song Sung Blue that inspired the 2025 Kate Hudson-Hugh Jackman film, explores exciting gains made in the biotech world and introduces us to groundbreakers making that happen, including San Francisco’s Celine Halioua, CEO/founder of a biotech startup that’s championing longevity medication that’s being tested on dogs. Kohs juxtaposes these encouraging developments with poignant testimonies from a close-knit community of residents in Maine whose stories suggest a positive outlook, an active lifestyle, and animal companions are key to making an extended lifespan worth living. —Randy Myers
