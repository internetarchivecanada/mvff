The Man I Love

From the festival

Play Trailer In late 1980s New York, Jimmy (Rami Malek), a theater artist living with AIDS, throws himself back into his work, rehearsing a new stage production while surrounded by lovers, family, friends, and fellow artists. Ira Sachs brings us into a world where fear and extraordinary creative energy exist side by side, capturing a generation determined to keep making art, falling in love, having sex, laughing, and living. Rather than turning the AIDS crisis into a conventional illness story, Sachs finds its presence in the relationships, silences, bodies, and choices of the people who lived through it. Malek gives Jimmy a magnetic, mercurial presence, while Tom Sturridge, Rebecca Hall, and Luther Ford bring intimacy, affection, and shifting loyalties into his world. Music becomes another language, with performances revealing what Jimmy cannot always say. Following Peter Hujar’s Day (MVFF48), Sachs returns to New York with a deeply sensual portrait of art, desire, community, and survival. —João Federici
