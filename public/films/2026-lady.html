Lady

From the festival

Filmed in the chaotic streets of Lagos, Nigeria, Olive Nwosu's first feature is a striking neo-noir that crackles with tension. One of the traffic-choked city’s few female cab drivers, Lady is independent, isolated, and mistrustful, surviving by staying laser-focused on her own business. Her foundation is shaken by the sudden reappearance of Pinky, the sister-like childhood friend who abandoned Lady years ago and became a sex worker. Tempted by the promise of money, time with Pinky, and a possible path out of a nation on the brink of political and social combustion, Lady reluctantly accepts a job driving Pinky’s crew of glamorous, reckless coworkers through the dangerous netherworld of Lagos nightlife. Writer-director Nwosu, fresh among Africa’s new wave of auteurs, shines with her debut. The cast, which won the Special Jury Award for Acting Ensemble at Sundance, is phenomenal, crafting a moving portrayal of the unexpected sisterhood that finally breaches Lady’s armor. —D. Quinones
