Red Boat Crossing/The Ship That Turned Back

From the festival

Bay Area directors create warmly emotional odes to family members’ WWII travails. Allie Light’s The Ship That Turned Back chronicles late spouse Irving Saraf’s childhood escape from occupied Poland; Jeanne C. Finley’s Red Boat Crossing recalls her mother’s dramatic Red Cross service.

Directed by
Jeanne C. Finley, Allie Light
