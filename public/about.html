About this explorer

An index of the Mill Valley Film Festival's films, people and programmes, built from the festival's own printed programmes and websites as they survive in the Wayback Machine and online.

Where the film lists come from

  • 2001–2011: the festival's printed programmes, saved as PDFs from cafilm.org and mvff.com in the Wayback Machine. Claude subagents read every page of each programme into structured records (title, directors, countries, year, running time, section, screenings, guests, tributes and events). The one-line film descriptions on those years' pages are written by Claude, not copied from the programme; each film page links to the archived programme it came from.
  • 2023–2024: the programme data embedded in mvff.com's "browse the program" page, read from Wayback Machine captures taken during each festival.
  • 2025–2026: the festival app at app.mvff.com, film by film, from the Wayback Machine where captured and from the live site otherwise. The short descriptions on these pages are the festival's own one-line loglines.
  • Other years link to the festival's scans of its programme guides (on Issuu, via mvff.com's guide archive), but their films are not in the explorer yet.

Printed programmes disagree with themselves now and then (dates, spellings, running times); each year page has the reading notes. Names are as printed; different people with the same name are merged.

Web archives

We checked every page, film record and document on mvff.com and cafilm.org, and every programme guide, against the Wayback Machine and saved what had never been captured; see What's archived.

Accessibility

Pages are plain HTML with headings, landmarks, a skip link and visible focus, work without JavaScript (except filtering and search), and follow the system's light or dark setting. If something does not work for you, email info@archive.org.
