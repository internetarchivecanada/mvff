What the Wayback Machine holds

The festival has lived on many websites since 2000: static film notes, a Drupal site, yearly subdomains, two ticketing systems and today's WordPress and festival app. Its printed programme guides, back to 1978, are now on Issuu. Here is what was online in November 2026, checked against the Wayback Machine's capture index, and what we did about the gaps.

Images (15,647 never captured on their own) are not sent separately: Save Page Now captures the images a page shows when it saves the page. We left out 21 non-PDF files from the sites' media libraries (drafts, documents, settings) and 81 PDFs that look internal or personal (sponsor decks, guest lists, job descriptions, release forms, login instructions, drafts).

On 18 November 2026 we sent 581 never-archived addresses to Save Page Now: 301 are now in the Wayback Machine, 255 could not be captured and 25 were still waiting when this page was built (2026-11-18 17:14).
