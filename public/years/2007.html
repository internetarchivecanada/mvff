Reading notes for this year's programme

PDF page numbers are used for 'page' (printed page numbers differ, e.g. PDF page 37 = printed p.83). Films A to Z (PDF pp.36-64) was cross-checked against the film calendar (pp.66-69) and the title index (p.85); no films missing. Opening/Centerpiece/Closing/Spotlight films are dated from their A-Z entries, with the event pages' extra screenings merged; 'guests' for these are the printed 'Invited Guests' (attendance not confirmed). Shorts programs (5@5 x6, Bee-ing Me, Bunnies and Butterflies, Dig-It-All!, The iGeneration Download, Outer and Inner Spaces, Tooned to Murder) are entered as one short per title with the program's screenings and shorts_program set; the program titles are not separate records. Shorts screened ahead of features ('Preceded by': The Headman and I, Cross Your Eyes Keep Them Wide, Common Enemies, Turn Back South, Learning to Fly, Salim Baba, Betty Boop for President) have is_short true, shorts_program null and the feature's screenings. Strong Love and Body & Soul: Diana & Kathy screened together as one double bill. Shorts summaries are brief, based on the program's one-line descriptions; iGeneration Download youth shorts had no individual descriptions, so they share a generic summary. 'Cinema' venue code (CARA08C, CASS08C, GONE09C) was read as Century Cinema, Corte Madera. Into the Wild was a pre-festival screening on September 13 (slot set to Spotlight). Countries/years are as printed; several entries omit the year (California Dreamin', In Search of a Midnight Kiss, Kenny). Language spellings kept as printed (e.g. 'Portugese', 'Pidgen'). The iGeneration short Dessert is credited to Max Strebel in the program note but to Ashlyn Perri in the filmmaker index. Only MVFF Awards are listed in awards; prizes films won at other festivals (Golden Lion, Silver Bear, Camera d'Or etc.) are not. PDF page 65 (printed p.111, 'The Tao of Film') is blank in the text. Sponsor, donor, membership, acknowledgment, print-source and advertiser pages were skipped; staff taken from Festival Staff (pp.72-73), omitting long lists of theater staff, projectionists and interns.
