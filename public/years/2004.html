Reading notes for this year's programme

Venue names in screenings: Rafael = Christopher B. Smith Rafael Film Center, Regency = Century Regency, Century Cinema = Century Cinema Corte Madera, Castro = Castro Theatre, 142 Throckmorton = 142 Throckmorton Theatre. The PDF holds only part of the printed programme (printed pages 1-160 with gaps; ads and many sponsor pages absent). Film 'section' uses the section lists on pp.65-68 (Official Premiere Selection preferred when a film is in two lists); films absent from every list have section null. Shorts programmes (5@5, The Dark Side of the 'Toon, The Best of Sports Shorts, Vidiot's Delight, youth reel, Let's Play, Top Hat and Tales) are split into individual short records sharing the programme's screenings; programme-level runtimes not stored. Merged duplicates: The Critical Path (5@5: I'm Movin' On / Vidiot's Delight); Ryan (The Dark Side of the 'Toon / Vidiot's Delight) (shorts_program keeps the first programme). Vera Drake's Oct 14 Regency screening comes from the calendar grid only. Companion shorts screened with features (Confessions of an Old Teddy, My Brother Nathaniel, Punjabi Cab, The Ladies' Room) carry the feature's screenings; The Ladies' Room (55 min) is not a short by runtime and is recorded as a feature. Citizen Stan and Professional Revolutionary screened as a double bill. Gumby Dharma (dir. Robina Marchesi, USA per indexes) appears only in the calendar grid (Oct 16 3pm Throckmorton) with no description; summary null. Hi De Ho Show has no credits or runtime. Four 'TBA Screening' slots on Oct 16-17 are not recorded. Uncertain readings: Story Undone's second date is printed 'Sunday Oct 13' (Oct 13 2004 was a Wednesday; the grid confirms Wednesday Oct 13). Grey Gardens credit prints 'Ellen Horde'; the essay and filmmaker index give Ellen Hovde (used). The Chorus director printed both 'Christopher' and 'Christophe' Barratier (Christophe used). Day Off the Dead co-director printed 'Jeffrey Davis' in the description and 'Jeffrey Dates' in the index. The title index lists 'Let's Play in Morocco' but the description and filmmaker index say Kosovo. Ganges Oct 15 code says 'S' but venue printed Raf. The Big Red One: The Reconstruction is dated 1980/2004 (film_year null); The Grateful Dead Movie is 1977/2004 (1977 used), its premiere is the restored re-release. Shorts' countries come from the Films by Country index where listed. I Heart Huckabees is printed 'I ` Huckabees' (heart glyph lost in extraction). Youth reel titles 'Urban Frankenstein and Bloodlust' treated as two shorts. Only 14 of the 15 youth-reel shorts are named. No festival awards are printed in this programme (only past awards such as Venice and Berlin prizes mentioned in film notes), so awards is empty. Staff list is from the Festival Staff page; board, sponsors, members and acknowledgments were skipped.
