Reading notes for this year's programme

Ads-removed PDF (88 pages); 'page' is the PDF page (=== PAGE n ===), not the printed page number (Films A to Z printed pp. 76-102 = PDF 41-67). Screening venues abbreviated: Sequoia = CinéArts@Sequoia, Rafael = Christopher B. Smith Rafael Film Center, Century Cinema = 'Cinema' (CIN) in listings. Opening/Closing/Tribute films merged with their A-Z entries (A-Z page used). Shorts programmes (5@5, Brink of Life, Découpage Digital, Explore, The Home in My Heart, Lost Souls and Malcontent Beasties, Lessons in Lice Language and Lipstick, You Can't Do That!, The Amazing Osamu Tezuka, Butterflies companions) are represented only by their individual shorts, which carry the programme's screenings; shorts that preceded a feature have shorts_program 'Preceding <feature>'. Rex Barrett and the Eye of God preceded The Lost Skeleton Returns Again on Oct 5 only. Laban the Little Ghost (44 min) screened with The Snow Queen and is not marked short. Index-derived items: the 16 You Can't Do That! titles and directors, the Youth Reel directors/countries, the five Magnus Renfors titles, Astro Boy: Expedition to Mars, TYPECAST's credited makers, and 'Self Portrait' come only from the Films by Filmmaker / Title / Print Sources / Films by Country indexes; the index places the Renfors titles on printed p.102 (You Can't Do That!) but the Brink of Life synopsis names Renfors, so they are assigned to Brink of Life (uncertain); 'Self Portrait' (Japan, Les Films du Paradoxe) is assumed part of the Tezuka programme (uncertain); summaries for most You Can't Do That! shorts are generic because the programme note does not name which film is which. Not added as films: 'Wherever You Are' (US country list only, no other trace) and three untitled Josh Safdie shorts shown after The Pleasure of Being Robbed; Fujian Blue's two parts (The Neon Knights; At Home, At Sea) are kept within Fujian Blue. Four Seasons Lodge Oct 11 printed '12:00 am' in its entry; calendar says 12:00 PM (used). Learning Gravity has no year printed. Hi De Ho Show has no credits; runtime 120 from calendar. Slumdog Millionaire was a 'Late Addition' with no section. Two 'TBA' calendar slots (Oct 11 7:15 pm Rafael, Oct 12 8:30 pm Sequoia) skipped. Garbled characters fixed (e.g. Jiří Macháček, Nataša Burger in Teddy Bear; Danuta Stenka in Katyn; Hrithik Roshan printed 'Mrithik' in Jodhaa Akbar credits). Cover text page 1 is font-garbled. Screening committee, theater staff, interns, board, donors, sponsors, acknowledgments and CFI member lists skipped.
