Reading notes for this year's programme

Page numbers are PDF page markers (printed page = roughly PDF page + 10 in the film notes). Screening venues use the programme's short names (Sequoia = CinéArts@Sequoia, Rafael = Christopher B. Smith Rafael Film Center, 142 Throckmorton = 142 Throckmorton Theatre, Century Cinema printed as 'Cinema'); the Oct 12 Storm screening at Century Cinema is a members' screening. Opening/Closing Night, Tribute and Spotlight listings were merged into the films' own entries. Precious is listed US 2009 on the Opening Night page but US 2008 in its film entry; 2008 used. Pierrot le fou has no year in its entry; 1965 taken from the synopsis. Croupier's director (Mike Hodges) comes from the Clive Owen essay; it has no film-notes entry. Featurettes shown with a feature ('Preceded by': Inside Story, Hidden Bounty of Marin, Shadow & Light, Love Child, Dragonflies the Baby Cries, The Marina Experiment, The Faeries of Farthingale) are is_short with shorts_program null and share the feature's screenings; Ingelore (40 min, preceding Shylock) is kept as a non-short. A Thousand Suns, Mustang – Journey of Transformation and Trading Bows and Arrows for Laptops screened together as one programme. [BLANK.] is the 96-minute youth reel of 19 films; 12 titles are named on its page and 7 more (Art?, Don't Waste Water, Harrison Bergeron, I'm Not My Tights, Marin School of the Arts PSA, Shooting Craps, The Natural World) appear only in the title/country indexes pointing to that page; none has director or runtime printed. 'The Sylpphid' spelled as printed in the indexes. Hi De Ho Show (live veejayed clip show hosted by John Goddard) kept as a film since it has a film-notes entry; A Sweeter Music (live concert, also in the film notes) is recorded as an event only. Active Cinema: Strategies for Change is 12:30 pm in New Movies Lab and the calendar but 2:30 pm on the Active Cinema page; 12:30 used. Awards list only the MVFF awards presented at the tributes/spotlights (Anna Karina's tribute postponed to spring 2010); prizes from other festivals mentioned in film notes are not included. Late additions Sweet Rush and The Road come from PDF page 69. Skipped: sponsors, donors, board, CFI members, print sources, ticketing, hospitality lounge/Outdoor Art Club café hours, screening committee and theater/intern staff lists.
