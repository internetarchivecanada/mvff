Reading notes for this year's programme

Source is only the alphabetical film-notes section (printed pages 25-50) of the 2002 programme; front matter, schedule grid, Opening Night (printed p.18) and Closing Night (printed p.24) pages are not in this file, so festival date range, venue addresses (except Rafael Film Center), staff and festival awards are absent. Edition 25 taken from repeated '25th Anniversary' references. Screening dates fall Oct 3-13, 2002; Frida's opening-night and Merci Docteur Rey's closing-night screenings are not dated here, only their second screenings. Venue 'Cinema' is as printed (likely Century Cinema, not stated). Shorts programmes (Aardvark Adventures and Other Animania, Animarathon 2002, Animated Amigos, Cake Steak and Cheez Whiz, Even Oddfellows Get the Blues, the five Five@Five programmes, Heaven's Crossroad/Inside the Box, Human Rights and Inhuman Wrongs, The Northern De-Lights, A Scandinavian Salute, Two-by-Forman, Videosyncrasies 2002, We Are Family) are not film records themselves; each constituent film is, with shorts_program set and the programme's screenings copied; shorts shown before a feature have shorts_program = that feature's title. Constituent films of 40+ minutes in such programmes have is_short false. Runtimes printed as m:ss were truncated to whole minutes; 'Don't Let Your Friends Rot' and 'We're All Just People!' share a printed 2 min, runtime null. Shorts with no country printed take the programme country only when the programme is USA-only. Animated Amigos Oct 6 time printed '11:30PM' (probably 11:30AM), kept as printed; Just a Kiss Oct 3 time printed '9:45' without AM/PM. Farang Ba '(Crazy White Foreigner)' and Gagooman '(The Twilight)' parentheticals are English translations, not original titles, so original_title is null. Guests include 'Invited Guest' names. Guest name spellings normalised: 'Sarah Hanneman' -> Sarah Hannemann, 'Nicoloas Breviére' -> Nicolas Brevière, director 'Mahmound Ben Mahmound' -> Mahmoud Ben Mahmoud, 'Kairis̆s' -> Kairišs. These Are Not My Images year printed '2000/2001' so film_year null; Cake, Steak and Cheez Whiz printed 'USA 2001/2002'. Section 'Children's FilmFest' is assigned only to I Was a Rat (explicitly labelled); many other entries say 'Recommended for ages N and up' but no section is printed. Awards printed only as past honours (e.g. Golden Bear for Bloody Sunday, Camera d'Or) are not festival awards and were omitted. Reviewers are credited only by initial and surname, so no staff list was built. Independent Spirits also includes unnamed Hubley shorts; A Year Along the Abandoned Road has no director printed.
