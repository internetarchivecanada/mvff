Reading notes for this year's programme

The archived PDF (38 pages) lacks the Film Descriptions section (printed pages 28-52) and PDF pages 3, 7 and 27 are empty or ads, so most films have no year, runtime, cast or synopsis; summaries are null wherever the programme gives no description. Films were assembled from the section lists (PDF pp. 8, 10-15), the Opening/Closing/Tribute pages (20-28), the Calendar (29-32), the Encore page (33) and the Index (37); shorts known only from the Index get page 37 and their parent programme from the Index's parenthesis. Screenings come from the multi-column Calendar, untangled by ticket codes (S=Sequoia, R=Rafael, O=OAC, M=Masonic, C=Pacific Cinema Corte Madera); they agree with the explicit dates on the VFest and Children's FilmFest pages. Three TBA calendar slots (Oct 9 9:30 PM Sequoia, Oct 10 9:00 PM Rafael, Oct 14 4:45 PM Rafael) were skipped. Films listed in several sections were given one section in this priority: Children's FilmFest, Five@Five, Valley of the Docs, New Media/VFest, Official Premiere Selection, World Cinema focus (Latin America / Iran), U.S. Cinema, World Cinema. Countries 'USA' for U.S. Cinema titles and 'Iran' for Migratory Goose are inferred from the section heading. The Mystic Masseur is 'India' in the World Cinema list but 'USA' in its own Opening Night entry (used). Lantana is 'Australia/Germany' in the list but 'Australia' in its entry (used). For shorts screened with a feature, shorts_program is the feature/programme name as in the Index. Double bills (Judy's Time / My Mother India, The Hotel Upstairs / The Landfill, The Disenchanted Forest / The Shaman's Apprentice) are kept as separate non-short records. 'Return to Sender' appears in both Sons Set in South Africa and Who Needs Love; kept as two records since it is unclear whether it is the same film. Five@Five short 'Elvis Doesn't Sing Here Anymore' is 'Elvis Doesn't Sing Anymore' in the Index. Director names kept as printed (e.g. 'Rakhshan Bani Elemad', 'Amir Ali Abdollah zadeh'). Invited guests (Michael Badalucco, Barbara Hershey, Anthony LaPaglia, Malcolm McDowell for encore screenings) are listed as guests. Seminar 'The Future of the Art Film' is 10AM-12NOON on its page but 11:00 in the Calendar (page time used); 'Animation: What's Up, Doc?' is misprinted 'Saturday October 3 5PM' on its page, Calendar shows Oct 13 3:00 (used). Kids Rock 'n' B-Roll and The Mo' Hi De Ho Show are compilation programmes with no individual titles printed. Encore screenings run after the festival (Oct 15-18). Tribute reception times differ slightly between the Special Events page (p.4) and the tribute pages; tribute pages used. No festival awards are printed in this programme.
