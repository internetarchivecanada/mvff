Reading notes for this year's programme

Page numbers are PDF pages (=== PAGE n ===); printed page numbers are about 6 higher. Grid codes: 'Rafael'=Christopher B. Smith Rafael Film Center, 'Sequoia'=CinéArts@Sequoia, 'Throck'/'142 Throckmorton'=142 Throckmorton Theatre, 'Cinema'=Century Cinema (Corte Madera), 'Larkspur'=Cinemark Larkspur Landing. Guitar Man screenings are printed as '742 Throckmorton' in the film note (grid says throck); normalised to 142 Throckmorton Theatre. Printed weekday errors: Principles 'Friday, October 13' and The Welcome 'Friday, October 16' kept as 2011-10-13 and 2011-10-16 (dates match the grid). Shorts that precede a feature (Mr. Happy Man, While You Were Gone, American Homes, Gold Violin: Bow of Death, A Woman's Descent to the Sacred, Miss South Pacific: Beauty and the Sea, The Walk, Library of Dust) and Right to Play (screens with The Real Rocky) are is_short=true with shorts_program=null and the feature's screenings. Shorts programmes recorded via shorts_program: the five 5@5 programmes, The Barber, the Brush and the Baton, Our New Frontier: Sustainability, and the Youth Reel; Hip Hop Maestro (41 min) counted as short because it is in a shorts programme. Youth Reel titles (beyond Ndapewa and Alone, named in the note) come only from the title index; no directors, countries or runtimes printed. A further CFI My Place, My Story film in the Youth Reel is described but not named. Hi De Ho Show (John Goddard's jazz clip evening, listed among film notes under US Cinema) recorded as a music event, not a film. The grid shows Play Like a Lion at 142 Throckmorton on Friday Oct 14, 7:30 PM (AKBR14T) and 'Play Like a Lion (Live)' on Sat Oct 15 with the same code; the concert page gives Sat Oct 15, 7:30 PM (AKBR15T), so the Oct 14 grid cell was treated as a misplacement and not added as a film screening. Grid lists One Through Love at 120 mins (note says 59 mins, programme includes a live web dialogue); runtime kept at 59. Grid slots marked TBA not recorded. Day of the Flowers' Oct 11 screening is the free Friends of the Festival screening for members. Active Cinema panel time was printed as TBA in the grid (2:00 PM on panel page). Staff: no festival staff list is printed; screening committee members (thank-you list) and two named CFI/MVFF roles are given. Program-note writers not recorded.
