Reading notes for this year's programme

Page numbers are PDF pages (=== PAGE n ===); printed page numbers run about 5 higher. Screening venues use the programme's codes: Rafael = Christopher B. Smith Rafael Film Center, Sequoia = CinéArts@Sequoia, 142 Throckmorton = 142 Throckmorton Theatre, Cinema = the 'C' venue code (probably Century Cinema, Corte Madera, but the grid only says CINEMA). Dates printed as 'October 7–17' (festival week) with the year from the cover; edition 33 from '33rd Mill Valley Film Festival'. Shorts programmes (5@5 x5, Wangypong youth programme, Truth Be Told, The Jay Clay Trilogy) are not records themselves; each short carries the programme's screenings and its name in shorts_program. Shorts that 'precede' a feature have shorts_program set to that feature's title. Programme runtimes: Gimme Some Truth 65, Just Like Starting Over 58, Nobody Told Me 61, Watching the Wheels 64, Working Class Hero 70, Wangypong 96, Truth Be Told 77, Jay Clay Trilogy 27 (90 in the calendar grid). Wangypong youth shorts have titles only (no director/country/runtime); titles follow the title index (Wangypong the Movie, Decision Day: The Musical, Green Profile Series: Franceyez, White Walls); the index also spells 'Git Along, Little Dogies' and 'Evolution of War'. The Quiet Life screened both in 5@5: Watching the Wheels and in The Jay Clay Trilogy; merged into one record. Children's FilmFest overview (p17) pairs The Indian with Syawal Was Very Scared and Twigson with The Silk Road, but the film notes pair The Indian with The Silk Road and Twigson with Syawal; the film notes were followed. The Girl Who Kicked the Hornet's Nest entry prints director 'David Alfredson'; the Focus list prints 'Daniel Alfredson', used here. The King's Speech is 'UK 2010' on the Opening Night page but 'UK/Australia 2009' in its film note (note used). My Brothers' second screening is printed Friday Oct 15 6:30 pm with ticket code MYBR16S (calendar shows MYBR15S). Cinemasports intro meeting is Sunday Oct 10 9 am on p51 but Saturday Oct 9 9 am on p52; event records the Oct 10 8 pm public screening. Nuremberg is dated 'US 1948/2009' (film_year 1948). Golf in the Kingdom is billed 'Sneak Preview' (stored in premiere). Hi De Ho Show is a 120-min clip show hosted by John Goddard, kept as a film. Submission is billed North American Premiere in its note but absent from the premieres list. Calendar also lists unnamed TBA slots (Oct 9 12:15 Rafael; Oct 10 7:15 Sequoia; Oct 11 9:30 Sequoia; Oct 16 4:00 and 6:00 Rafael; Oct 17 5:00, 8:30, 7:30 Rafael). The Global Lens titles other than Adrift and Becloud screen after the festival (Oct 18–28) and were not included. No festival staff list is printed (programme-note writers are credited by name only), so staff is empty. Only the four MVFF honorary awards are recorded; other awards mentioned in film notes were won elsewhere.
