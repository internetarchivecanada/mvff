Reading notes for this year's programme

PDF page numbers are used (printed page = PDF page - 2). Festival dates are not printed as a single range; 'October 6-16, 2005' is taken from the Opening Night (Thursday Oct 6) and Closing Night (Sunday Oct 16) listings and the movie calendar. Short films are taken from the 5@5 programmes, the shorts programmes (Different Drummers, Tender Gender Benders, A Toon for the Misbegotten, Let the Children Lead, Short Films for Little People) and 'Preceded by' shorts; preceding shorts carry the feature's screenings and shorts_program null. The Sweet and Sick youth reel (Oct 15 11 AM Rafael, 74 min) prints no titles; the 15 shorts listed under it are inferred from the title index, which places these otherwise undescribed titles on the Sweet and Sick page. 'premiere' = 'Official Premiere Selection' for films on that printed list (the programme gives no per-film US/world premiere labels), plus 'Western US Premiere' (The Fakir) and 'World Premiere' (Sound of the Soul, from the concert blurb). The Sleeping Child is listed as 'Yasmine Kassan, Morocco' on the premiere list but 'Yasmine Kassari, Belgium/Morocco' in its entry; the entry is used. Spellings kept as printed: 'Taiki Waititi' / 'Tama Tü' (index: Tama Tü), 'La Majique Noire' (index: La Magique Noire), 'Messiah' (index: The Messiah). 'Train and Truck' is treated as one film per its description, though the index lists Train and Truck separately. Soul of Justice credits Rick Goldsmith as director but the synopsis names 'local filmmaker Abby Ginzberg'; the credit line is used. Innocent Voices (Friends of the Festival screening) and Prime screen at Century Cinema, Corte Madera. The Hi De Ho Show (John Goddard's 1960s music-video programme) is kept as a film entry since it is listed in Films A to Z. The Opening Night Gala box misprints 'Thursday Oct 7'; Oct 6 is used. Several calendar slots read 'TBA'. No MVFF awards are printed in this programme (only other festivals' prizes in synopses), so awards is empty. No festival staff list is printed; staff holds the contributing writers and the CFI Outreach manager. Post-festival Powell and Jeunet retrospective titles at the Rafael (page 48) are not festival screenings and are not included as films.
