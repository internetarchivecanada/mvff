Reading notes for this year's programme

Calendar pages (PDF 25-27), the sponsor/venue logo pages and several other pages are images with no text; screenings come from the Film Notes entries (pages 13-24). The 'Films by Category' pages (8-10) list films in columns whose category headings are images and were not captured, so 'section' is only set where the text says so: Gala (Opening/Centerpiece/Closing), Children's FilmFest (features marked 'Ages N+' and listed together in the children's column; kids' shorts programmes with age ratings), and 5@5. Shorts programmes are recorded via each short's shorts_program (Roof Sex and Violet Lives Upstairs screened in two programmes, joined with '; '); shorts 'Shown with' a feature have shorts_program null and the feature's screenings. Short runtimes/years are not printed (only programme totals). Alex's Wedding (45 min) and Si-Guériki (63 min) screen together as one programme. The Hi De Ho Show (John Goddard video/music evening) is recorded as an event, not a film. Film guests: 'In person', 'Introduced by' and gala 'Invited Guest' lines only; tribute-dinner invited guests are on the events. No awards, premiere labels or festival staff are printed; staff lists the programme's contributing writers. Edition 26 from 'Issue #26' and 'the 26th annual Mill Valley Film Festival'; dates derived from the Oct 2 opening and Oct 12 closing nights and 'October 2-October 12' box-office hours (no date range is printed as a headline). Conflicting spellings kept from film notes: Burnt Sienna is credited to Tolan Harber in notes and on one category page but to Joey Curtis on another; Eldra director 'Tim Lin' (notes) vs 'Tim Lyn' (category); The Klarsfelds 'Elisabeth' vs 'Elizabeth' Citroën; Burials and Borders 'Paula' vs 'Paulo' Levine; Chess King Ali director 'Merial Uslu' (category) vs 'Meral Uslu' (notes); Djibril Diop Mambéty printed 'Djirbril' in the category list. Girl with a Pearl Earring is printed as 73 mins and its star as 'Scarlet Johansson' (kept as printed). Sam Elliot spelled as printed.
