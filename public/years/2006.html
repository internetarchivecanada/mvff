Reading notes for this year's programme

Feature descriptions are on PDF pages 35-64 (printed pp. 70-99); PDF pages 65-68 are the film calendar grid, which duplicates the descriptions and was used only to cross-check screenings (Tribute/Spotlight/seminar/music codes there are covered in events). Shorts programmes (5@5, Beneath the Southern Cross, Fuss Mess and Scrumble, Grown Up, Hyphy, Outside/In, Toon In Turn On Drop Dead, VidéOnze) are split into individual short-film records with shorts_program set; the programmes themselves are not separate film records. Companion films listed as 'Preceded by' are separate records; those under 40 min have is_short true and shorts_program 'Preceding <feature>'; longer companions (Toxic Bust 41 min, Working Title 54 min, The Glenmoore Job 52 min) are features with shorts_program null. Hi De Ho Show (a music-video programme presented by John Goddard) is kept as a film record because it is listed among the film descriptions. Directors for several Toon In / Grown Up / Fuss shorts (and co-director Michaela Eremiasova on All That Remains) come from the 'films by filmmaker' index (PDF pp. 80-81), whose multi-column layout was untangled by position; Heavy Metal Jr. director could not be determined. Fuss, Mess and Scrumble and Hyphy shorts have only titles and makers printed, so their summaries are generic. Premiere: films in the Official Premiere Selection list (p.34) carry 'Official Premiere Selection' unless the programme names a more specific premiere (Breaking and Entering and Hinokio US Premiere; The Astronaut Farmer World Premiere). Text discrepancies: Breaking and Entering year printed '200' on p.12 and 2005 on p.39 (2005 used); index spellings differ for some shorts (Propellerbird, There Was the Moon and a Fox, Blood of Yingzhou District, Taylor Broek) - description spellings used. Men at Work 142 Throckmorton code printed MEN08T/MENA08T. Screening venue short names: Sequoia = CinéArts@Sequoia, Rafael = Christopher B. Smith Rafael Film Center, 142 Throckmorton = 142 Throckmorton Theatre, Century Cinema (Corte Madera), Embarcadero = Embarcadero Center Cinema. Awards: only the MVFF tribute and spotlight honours named on p.3; an Audience Award ballot is mentioned but no winners are printed. Staff: ambiguous column pairings on PDF p.69 (e.g. CFI Outreach Manager, Programmer, Youth Workshop Coordinator, Special Events Assistant, Outdoor Art Club Manager) were omitted; interns/theater staff/projectionists not listed. PDF page 1 is unreadable (cover glyphs); page 22 is blank apart from a footer.
