Mill Valley Film Festival Explorer

Forty-nine years of the Mill Valley Film Festival in Marin County, California, from its first weekend at the Sequoia Theatre in 1978 to 2026: the films, the filmmakers who came, the tributes, and the programme guides that recorded them, with what the Wayback Machine has saved of it all.
